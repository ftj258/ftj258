## About

I got into digital forensics because I like puzzles where the evidence is already there — you just have to go find it. These days that's phishing campaigns, compromised accounts, and systems that quietly stopped behaving right. I follow evidence until the story makes sense. For seven years that's meant incident response and digital forensics mostly as a federal contractor: triaging alerts, leading incident response, building the CSIRT documentation and testing programs the team actually uses. It started with a DHS Cyber Crime Center internship and graduate research at George Mason University, and I've been chasing that same kind of problem ever since - the same thing that got me into it: a problem where the answer is sitting in the data, if you're willing to go find it!
